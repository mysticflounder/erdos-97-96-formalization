## First missing Lean bridge

Do **not** add another distance-transitivity lemma.  The existing bank already
expresses arbitrary row-equality chains.  The first useful Lean obligation is
a producer, schematically:

```lean
theorem exists_exactOffCircleCore_of_retainedOmission_triApexAllLarge
    (R : FrontierCommonDeletionParentResidual F)
    (Q : RetainedOmissionAllLargeNormalForm P)
    (G : TriApexAllLargeContext D S) :
    ∃ (α : Type) (Prows : EqualityCore.RowPattern α)
      (pointOf : α → ℝ²) (core : EqualityCore.ExactOffCircleCore Prows),
      EqualityCore.Realizes Prows pointOf ∧
      EqualityCore.ExactAt Prows pointOf core.c
```

The final interface may instead use `CarrierLabel D.A`,
`FaithfulCarrierPattern`, `exactAt_blocker`, and the critical-shell adapter.
The material requirement is the same: derive from the live `R/Q/G` data a
covering disjunction of actual row memberships whose equality closure reaches
an excluded label.  The v16 assignments witness ten examples of that terminal
shape but do not prove the disjunction.

If the producer is pursued only for the exact-15 lane, it must be stated
loudly with `D.A.card = 15` and the exact apex-profile hypotheses, followed by
a certified exhaustive Boolean cover.  Applying such a result to the present
universal target would still require a separate exact-15 reduction.  Until
one of those producer routes exists, the honest route verdict is **NO-GO for
promotion** despite the **GO for bank reuse**.
