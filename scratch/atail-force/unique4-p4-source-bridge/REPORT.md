## Direct row/arc finite seam

`P4DirectOuterArcFinite.lean` proves, without native computation, that every
valid finite datum converts to the geometric `DirectRowArcSchema` expected by
`direct_row_pattern_implies_outer_arc`.  The generated package supplies 3,179
such datums in 25 chunks (24 chunks of 128 and one of 107).

The aggregate theorem states:

```lean
theorem directRowArcFiniteData_batch_valid :
    directRowArcFiniteData.length = directRowArcFiniteDataCount ∧
      ∀ datum ∈ directRowArcFiniteData, datum.Valid
```

The integrity check authenticated the checked-in package with:

- aggregate Lean SHA-256
  `29092974cb10e465dc1196f3c60844bf45c1062fc7ade541d148c0777717f9b9`;
- semantic-data SHA-256
  `845398403dd0b206c01e1fb57b3f846b902bb541c85d591a1275ffb3aaaccfde`;
  and
- stable manifest digest
  `c407e22fa7d3b1c94e50cd4e11962df3848b29a5bc0f2809e1d9f8f22247cfb8`.

This is an **authenticated source-to-finite-datum materialization** plus a
Lean batch check, not an UNSAT proof.  The Python `--verify-only` check
authenticates the generated bytes, chunk boundaries, provenance ledger,
semantic digest, and stable manifest digest; it does not move those external
provenance claims into the Lean kernel.

Each chunk proves its finite predicate with `native_decide`.  Consequently,
the printed axiom closure of the aggregate is exactly:

```text
[propext, Classical.choice, Lean.ofReduceBool, Lean.trustCompiler, Quot.sound]
```

Thus the 3,179 finite validity results are compiler-trusted native-decision
results.  They must not be described as axiom-free.  In contrast, the printed
closures of `DirectRowArcFiniteCertificate.toSchema` and
`DirectRowArcFiniteDatum.toSchema` are exactly:

```text
[propext, Classical.choice, Quot.sound]
```

The conversion from a valid datum to the geometric schema does not add the
native-decision trust boundary.

## Remaining closure boundary

`nonempty_directSource_or_mirrorPacket` consumes the production P4 packet
split.  It closes the direct packaging step and intentionally returns the
mirror packet unchanged.  The remaining mirror obligation is to authenticate
the reflection of ledger supports, sides, and turn masks before reusing the
same generic family theorems.

The blocker-row hook is exposed as
`DirectSource.blockerRow_support_eq_shell`; it uses the production
`CriticalShellSystem`/`selectedFourClass_support_eq_shell` path through
`Census554.blocker_row_support_eq_shell`.  Construction-time exact-class
alignment remains upstream in `CriticalShellSystem.overrideExactSelectedClass`
and `overrideExactSelectedClass_centerAt`; this adapter does not replace the
frontier's shell system.

No theorem in this lane replays a SAT/UNSAT certificate or composes these
source results with every stored compact clause.  No production theorem or
production `sorry` is closed by the scoped validation alone.
