## What the search rules out

The strong conjecture “every recent survivor is eliminated by one uniform
three-row five-point core” is false for this wave: seven of the 25 proof-backed
cuts require more than three selected rows, and source37 is structural rather
than five-point.  Several five-point cuts also need different cores on
different source orders.

The data therefore does not justify a single fixed labeled-core theorem or a
source-independent arbitrary-label symmetry theorem.  Any broader orbit
compression must first prove that the relabeling preserves the named Rigid221
roles and the frozen boundary-order interface.

## Missing producer and lift

The live-sorry bottleneck is upstream of the geometry consumer.  Progress on
the anchor requires a source theorem that turns the Rigid221 branch data into
one of these positive core families, together with complete placement/cell
coverage.  A useful contract has two distinct levels:

1. **Exact-12 producer/coverage.** Every source-safe normalized exact-12 row
   assignment belongs to a checked terminal cell, and every SAT survivor in
   that cell supplies a replayable order-covered positive core.
2. **Universal lift.** Every live source instance either directly supplies one
   of the same row-incidence cores or has a source-entitled exact-12
   subconfiguration preserving all hypotheses needed by the terminal consumer.

Neither level follows from the 25 cuts.  The current terminal-bank consumer can
consume a verified terminal UNSAT result for a fixed cell; it does not prove
terminality, coverage of all normalized placements, or the universal lift.

Accordingly, the next general-theorem search should target the source producer:
which residual cap/deletion hypotheses force the seven incidences of a
three-row core, or force a finite disjunction of order-covered cores?  Proving
another local five-point contradiction would duplicate existing infrastructure.
