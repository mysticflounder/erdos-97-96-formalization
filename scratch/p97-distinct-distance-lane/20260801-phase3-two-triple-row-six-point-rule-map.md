## Exact role and literal map

Choose six distinct labels appearing in forward cyclic order

`A <cyc B <cyc C <cyc D <cyc E <cyc F`.

For the Lean boundary adapter (`TwoTripleRowSixPointEuclideanObstruction.lean:
223-283`), reindex the authenticated CCW boundary so that

| Rule role | Lean index | Required Phase-3 facts |
|---|---|---|
| `A` | `ia` | `s[B,A]`, `s[F,A]` |
| `B` | `ib` | center of first row |
| `C` | `ic` | `s[B,C]` |
| `D` | `id` | `s[B,D]`, `s[F,D]` |
| `E` | `ie` | `s[F,E]` |
| `F` | `iff` | center of second row |

The two triple-row antecedents are exactly:

1. center `B`, points `{A,C,D}`: `BA=BC` and `BA=BD`;
2. center `F`, points `{A,D,E}`: `FA=FD` and `FA=FE`.

Thus the direct assignment antecedent is

`s[B,A] & s[B,C] & s[B,D] & s[F,A] & s[F,D] & s[F,E]`.

`sat_generate.py:239-253,255-259,411-426` allocates and decodes these `s`
literals.  `realize.metric_rows` (`realize.py:411-417`) turns each selected
support into an `exact=False` metric row.  This is sound because the theorem
uses positive same-center equalities only.  The fourth selected support point
in each Phase-3 row is irrelevant, and no off-support inequality may be
inferred.

The order antecedent is not assignment-visible as five successor literals.
The current assignment provides seven `f[x,i]` cap-membership facts.  They are
validated by `_cap_facts` and expanded by `_cap_orders_from_facts`
(`phase3_structural_cegar.py:1228-1279`) to exactly 24 compatible orders, with
no reflection quotient; `realize.cap_orders` independently constructs the
same order set (`realize.py:367-408`).

## Sound detection/admission rule

For each authenticated compatible order, rotate it cyclically and enumerate
six increasing positions.  A forward witness is a role tuple `(A,B,C,D,E,F)`
whose two direct support tests above hold.  One matching order is only an
order-leaf hit.  An assignment-level clause requires:

1. every order for the source seven `f` facts has a forward witness;
2. after cap-fact minimization, every full Phase-3 cap annotation extending the
   retained facts, and all 24 orders generated by each extension, has a
   witness;
3. the checker recomputes the witness and its six selected `s` memberships,
   rather than trusting stored roles or hashes;
4. only then negate the union of required `s` memberships and retained `f`
   facts.

Do **not** reuse `_compatible_cap_coverage` unchanged: at
`phase3_structural_cegar.py:1361-1367` it accepts either a forward or reversed
cyclic subsequence.  This Lean adapter proves the forward CCW case only.  Add
an explicit allowed-orientation policy and set it to `("forward",)` for this
rule.  A reversed hit needs a separately justified Lean consumer, not a Python
symmetry assumption.

The sharp direct witness uses six `s` literals.  The existing generic
whole-row clause (`_clause_for_rows`, lines 2299-2325) would use all eight
memberships of the two selected four-support rows.  That is sound but weaker.
Prefer a rule-specific checked membership projection, analogous to
`_duplicate_center_path_memberships` and `_clause_for_memberships`
(`phase3_structural_cegar.py:1120-1225,2665-2704`).  Across 24 orders the
certificate may need several witnesses, so its learned `s` antecedent is the
union of their replayed six-fact sets, not necessarily six literals total.

## Trust and false-positive boundary

- **Lean source:** the adapter and metric theorem are present and importable
  through `Phase3MetricCertificateBank.lean:7`.  `PHASE3-RESULTS.md:162-174`
  records an earlier focused build and transitive no-`sorryAx` audit, but this
  turn ran no Lean/Lake verification.  Current status here is source/LSP
  inspection only.
- **Python certificate:** hashes and replay establish deterministic Python
  evidence, not Lean kernel checking.  The theorem name must be described as a
  downstream consumer unless a concrete Lean certificate checker/coverage
  theorem is later added.
- **Order:** positive incidence without universal compatible-order coverage is
  the primary false-positive risk.  Reversal, a reflection quotient, or an
  unauthenticated cyclic rotation must not be smuggled in.
- **Metric:** `exact=False` permits positive same-row equalities only.  Never
  use absence of `s[p,q]`, blocker facts, or a fourth support point to derive a
  geometric inequality needed by this theorem.
- **Geometry:** the Lean adapter also assumes an injective CCW convex boundary.
  The rule is a conditional Euclidean non-realizability cut in that global P97
  consumer, not a pure propositional contradiction in the SAT variables.
