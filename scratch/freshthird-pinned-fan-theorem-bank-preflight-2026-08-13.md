# FreshThird pinned-fan theorem-bank preflight

## Frozen target

The bounded reuse pass was run only after the exact two-field contract
`FreshThirdPinnedEndpointOutsideSeedIncompatibilityContract` had been added to
`TwoSourceFreshThirdResidual.lean` and its reduction theorem had compiled.  The
contract requires a contradiction for every actual
`FreshThirdPinnedCenterBlockerSeparatedOutcome` and every actual
`FreshThirdPinnedNoCenterNormalizedOutcome`; it does not identify the fan's
repeated-cap pair, mutual-cross pair, or endpoint outside pair.

Query scope: source-clean, arbitrary-cardinality consumers preserving the full
pinned-endpoint packet and either

1. a nonalternating cyclic placement for the exact equal-actual-blocker fan
   pair, or
2. a transport of the exact mutual canonical-row cross omissions and distinct
   blockers for that same pair into a two-source/common-radius/endpoint
   terminal.

Rejected classes were fixed-cardinality, raw-W4, cap-only, anonymous-row, and
the target theorem itself.  The Lean corpus was indexed at source revision
`e1e67f9c`; the live worktree HEAD was
`91010b3e94f706ac3ee29b7dffa1c3ff745c52c0`.

## Results

The nearest matches were all producers already used by the normalization:

* `sourceFaithfulDeletionFan_of_triApexAllLargeContext` produces the fan but no
  cyclic placement or pair synchronization.
* `exists_mutualCrossDeletion_pair_of_sourceFaithfulFan_no_centerBlocker`
  produces an actual mutual-cross pair, but not a common-radius/endpoint
  transport for that pair.
* `centerBlocker_or_sourceFaithfulFanNoCenterBlockerRowWitness` is precisely the
  fan split already consumed by `normalizedOutcome`.
* `freshThird_pinnedEndpointRow_escape_or_offEndpoint` produces the endpoint
  branch and escaping row but does not align its selected outside pair with the
  repeated-cap or mutual-cross pair.
* `exists_distinct_outsideCap_fan_escape_or_crossDeletion` produces an actual
  outside pair with dependent deletion packets, but that pair can differ from
  both other existential pairs.

The apparent consumer
`false_of_freshThird_pinnedEndpoint_globalResidual` is circular for this
purpose: its proof invokes
`false_of_freshThird_pinnedEndpoint_outsideSeedResidual`, the open theorem being
reduced.  It is not a reusable contradiction.

## Verdict

No theorem-bank declaration constructs either field of the frozen
incompatibility contract.  The first missing antecedent is exact witness
synchronization plus cyclic/metric placement: `hrowAt` orders the named
FreshThird endpoint/source-center roles, not arbitrary fan sources or their
actual blocker centers.  The next admissible step is therefore an intrinsic
finite named-role query induced by the full packet and normalized witness,
with every existential pair retained separately and with no ambient-cardinality
bound.
