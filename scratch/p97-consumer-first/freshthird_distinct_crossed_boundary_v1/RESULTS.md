## Why no solver was run

The requested materially new finite object was a single shared-coordinate
model simultaneously coupling:

1. the three crossed exact rows and their exact support exclusions;
2. the cap/boundary cyclic packet;
3. the aligned singleton/rich class geometry; and
4. blocker exactness.

That is already the source contract of
`scratch/p97-consumer-first/freshthird_aligned_mutual_crossed_metric_v1/`.
Its preceding structural wave was dual-solver SAT with independently checked
models and malformed UNSAT controls, including instances negating the desired
positive producers.  Its metric refinements and the five-row/equality-path
v1--v5 followups then left source-faithful survivors and no complete adapter
to a named consumer.  Re-encoding the same fields here would not be CEGAR: it
would repeat the same abstraction without a new source-proved cut.

Consequently Z3/cvc5 readback and malformed-control requirements are not
applicable to this directory.  The validated evidence remains in the prior
campaigns; this directory makes no new solver claim.
