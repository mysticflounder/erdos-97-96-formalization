## CEGAR classification

The current pipeline is a **diagnostic structural sweep**, not source-faithful closure.  The wrapper enumerates 64 branch/name/arm/cap records; swapped `hnames` still lacks source-role adapters, and blocker partitions/per-row witnesses/deletion predicates remain absent.  The retained cross-cap blocker clause has the contrapositive shape of `crossDeletion_survives_of_distinct_sameCap_blockers_outside_pair`; final source-level soundness confirmation is pending.  The metric stage additionally omits source-exclusion/support disequalities, so SAT means only that this relaxation is satisfiable.  It is neither universal over the Lean source fan nor a fixed-survivor proof.

First enumerate the four `ORDER_ARMS`, with both possible `hnames` assignments (`qOutside/qBetween` swap), and branch `fresh_cap,row_cap` over the compatible non-first caps `{1,2}` while retaining the arm-imposed endpoint (`qBetween` for arms 1/3, `qOutside` for arms 2/4). Only after those are represented should cross-deletion predicates (both erased-row directions) be added; motif/shared-pair cuts cannot substitute for them.
