# Encoded / omitted ledger

## Encoded

- 48 labeled pair partitions modulo reversal (the six mirror-symmetric
  partitions are fixed points of reversal).
- 198 aliases after source inequalities, pairwise-distinct centers, and the
  strict-between gate.
- Six general planar strict-convex cap vertices represented by complete
  pair-radius rational directions; every increasing triple is CCW, with only
  translation/rotation/positive-scale gauges fixed.
- Three blocker pair equalities and three common-apex pair equalities.
- Three positive, pairwise-distinct common-apex squared radii.

## Omitted

- The carrier's other points and its total cardinality.
- Every anonymous K4 support row and all global row/blocker incidence.
- The full cap boundary and cyclic order outside the selected minor cap.
- The deletion-survival and canonical-row packets not represented by the six
  named points.
- Any theorem transporting the bounded aliases to arbitrary `n ≥ 15`.

The earlier unit-circle encoding was not source-entitled and is retracted; its
UNSAT counts are not evidence.  An omitted item may only weaken the geometric model if the finite abstraction
is used as an obstruction search; it cannot turn a bounded result into a
universal theorem.
