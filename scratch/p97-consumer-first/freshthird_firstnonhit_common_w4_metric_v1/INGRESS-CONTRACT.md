# Ingress contract

## Live source consequences

The common-radius `sameBlocker × sameBlocker` branch supplies:

- a common-radius two-cap-source canonical surface;
- a FreshThird blocker fiber `Q`;
- equality of both canonical source blocker centers with the blocker center of
  `Q.source₁`;
- equality of both canonical source selected supports with the selected
  support at `Q.source₁`;
- localization of that common blocker center in the strict canonical cap;
- exact canonical-cap trace of the common selected support equal to the two
  canonical sources; and
- at least four strict canonical-cap carrier points outside that support, each
  with deletion survival at the common blocker center.

The last four points form a source-entitled four-point set `W`.  The source
theorem does **not** assert that all points of `W` lie on one common radius, that
their actual blockers agree, or that their actual selected rows meet the
`Q` row.

## Frozen exact-17 representative

The wave additionally fixes one incidence representative:

- carrier labels and boundary rank exactly as serialized in `results.json`;
- exact closed caps of sizes `8, 6, 6`;
- strict canonical cap `{0,1,4,5,6,7}` with canonical source pair `{0,1}` and
  `W = {4,5,6,7}`;
- exact named selected supports at blocker-center labels `7`, `8`, and `9`;
- the `W` labels are assigned to the retained `P`/`Pρ` endpoint roles; and
- the canonical, `P`, and `Pρ` strict-cap pairs are constrained to three
  positive pairwise-distinct apex radii.

These label assignments are frozen-case assumptions.  They are not a theorem
that every live W4 packet has this form.

## Stages

1. `geometry_core`: strict convex boundary order, disk containment, and the
   nonobtuse Moser triangle.
2. `cap_classification`: exact membership in the three frozen closed caps.
3. `canonical_three_radii`: the three exact strict-cap radial slices and their
   positive pairwise-distinct radii.
4. `selected_row_equalities`: equal-distance equations for the three named
   exact-four supports.
5. `blocker_exactness`: off-support inequalities for those three named rows.

## Deliberately omitted

- arbitrary pointwise `D.K4` witness supports;
- anonymous deletion packets and universal minimality;
- FreshThird constructor arms other than this frozen common-radius
  `sameBlocker × sameBlocker` representative;
- general carrier cardinality; and
- any bounded-obstruction or finite-to-universal lifting theorem.
