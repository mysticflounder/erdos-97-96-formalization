The matched Lean theorem is the outside-pair uniqueness obstruction: if two centers are distinct but belong to the same ordered cap, they cannot both be the center of the same outside pair. In other words, a pair of support points outside the cap cannot be equidistant to both centers (equivalently, they cannot both bisect that same pair).

A second formulation says the same thing explicitly as an “outside pair” uniqueness theorem for ordered caps.
