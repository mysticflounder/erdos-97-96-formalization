The proof receives the accepted Form A v1, Form B v1, Form C v1, and Form B v2 exclusions as
arguments. It destructures the accepted cyclic-forms conjunction to obtain the five remaining
Form A, Form B, and Form C exclusions. The extracted N4c form split classifies every escaping
witness class at each distinguished vertex; the extracted N4d constructors then combine each
classification with its three form exclusions. Finally, `n4eCapContainment_of_n4c_n4d` packages
the three N4d predicates into `S.N4eCapContainment`.

The extraction is deterministic. `generate.py` authenticates the source fragment by its byte
count and SHA-256, authenticates the parent Form-B split artifact, rebuilds the parent stream to
authenticate the 52,954-byte N4e tail, and selects declarations by exact names. The retained
middle-tail helper uses the accepted `n4d_formb_branch` support record; the final wrapper uses
public accepted theorem modules for its five input facts.
