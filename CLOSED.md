# Solved negatively — project closure record

**Status:** Erdős Problems 96 and 97 are solved negatively. Project closed by the owner on 2026-09-13.

The Erdős 96/97 formalization project is closed. The owner authorized closure
on the basis of the new counterexample. The complete upstream Lean source and
exact mission adapters subsequently passed local kernel checks, and Prove2Me
accepted disproofs of both original statements and the combined goal. The
transfer was independently audited; no separate prose re-proof was undertaken.

Source authors: Liam Kruer, Jensen Kohlmeyer, and Liam Price.

Paper: [96-97.pdf](https://github.com/Leeham06972452/erdos-96-97/blob/0e98f5f9bdaf36007e3eb405cbefe2eda778a9b2/96-97.pdf)

Negative results: Ω(n log log n) edges and arbitrary minimum unit
degree in strictly convex polygons, together with the general fixed-k P97
statement.

The pinned source, accepted submissions, local checks, and closure receipts are at
[prove2me/submissions/negative-resolution-20260913/README.md](prove2me/submissions/negative-resolution-20260913/README.md).

Existing repository material remains preserved as historical partial work. New
P96/P97 positive-proof work, solver waves, and swarm dispatch require explicit
reopening by Adam.
