## Authority and supersession policy

The live authority is `docs/erdos-97-descent-prose-proof-atomic.md`, read with the current dated lane audits. The archive is evidence, not an instruction to concatenate manuscripts. **authoritative/current** is the selected lane checkpoint; **evidence-only** may support a status or calculation but is not copied into proof prose; **subsumed** is historical, duplicate, or superseded and remains provenance only. Patches, worksheets, solver outputs, validation logs, archives, and exports cannot promote a theorem.

Precedence is canonical atomic manuscript; latest F1 checkpoint 4/D1-v23, F2 checkpoint 36, F3 third pass F3.55-F3.73, and B-family v4; then literature and status ledgers; then older plans, patches, exports, and diagnostics.
