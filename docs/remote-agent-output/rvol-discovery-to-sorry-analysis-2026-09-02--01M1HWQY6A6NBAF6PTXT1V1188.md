## 2. Discovery inventory and exact Lean relevance

### 2.1 Arbitrary-cardinality incidence countermodel

For the cyclic carrier `ZMod n`, define

\[
U(c)=c+\{1,2,4,8\},\qquad \beta(q)=q-1.
\]

For `n ≥ 9`, this gives:

- four points in every row;
- center exclusion;
- a total source-to-row section with `q ∈ U(β(q))`;
- distinct-row intersection at most two;
- pair-codegree at most two;
- no nonempty proper support-closed subset.

For `n ≥ 15`, the two incidence bounds improve to one.

**What this proves strategically.**  A-ROW, a blocker section, support connectivity, row-intersection bounds, and pair-codegree bounds cannot close any arbitrary-cardinality RVOL leaf. This is worth formalizing off-spine as a negative theorem, but it is not a terminal for a live sorry.

### 2.4 Ordered-cap pair capacity and the missing equality packet

Existing source theorem:

`Problem97.CapSelectedRowCounting.orderedCap_card_add_four_le_choose_outside`.

It proves

\[
|C|+4\leq \binom{|A\setminus C|}{2}.
\]

The proof already contains the ingredients needed for an equality theorem:

- every cap-centered row has at least two outside points;
- the first and last cap rows have at least three outside points;
- each row contributes a family of outside pairs;
- outside-pair uniqueness makes these families pairwise disjoint;
- their union is contained in all pairs of outside points.

Under equality, one should extract:

1. endpoint outside-hit cardinalities exactly three;
2. every other cap-row outside-hit cardinality exactly two;
3. equality between the union of row-owned pairs and `(A \ C).powersetCard 2`;
4. existence and uniqueness of an owner cap center for every outside pair;
5. endpoint owner fibers of size three and all other owner fibers of size one.

This is a genuine new arbitrary-cardinality theorem. It is not currently the final hypothesis of a live sorry, but it is the correct source producer for future cap-extremal/defect-four reductions.

### 2.5 Reflection orders and abstract-metric independence

The probe constructed defect-four pair-owner reflection orders that avoid every local three-row Kalmanson triangle. For `n=17,24,32,41`, these were completed to exact rational finite metrics satisfying:

- all selected-row equalities;
- every triangle inequality;
- every strict K1 and K2 Kalmanson inequality.

The construction was further extended to abstract all-center systems with:

- a four-point row at every center;
- total source-indexed exact four-fibers containing the source;

- deletion of each source destroying every four-point fiber at its selected blocker;
- row-intersection and pair-codegree bounds;
- support strong connectivity;
- shared-pair separation;
- seven-source mutual omission.

Exact Cayley–Menger checks show that the displayed metrics are nonplanar.

**Consequence for the live proof.**  The following strategy class is inadequate:

```text

+ blocker/deletion axioms
+ support closure
+ seven-source omission

=> False.
```

Planar Euclidean realizability, rank-two distance geometry, or genuine MEC/cap provenance must enter. This is directly relevant to FreshThird, FirstNonHit, TriApex, and generic TwoSource planning.

### 2.6 Euclidean-only theorem candidates

The abstract countermodels fail to encode several genuinely planar phenomena found by the probe.

#### Sharp-turn budget

If `a,b,c` are consecutive boundary vertices and a selected row centered at `a` contains `b,c`, then `|ab|=|ac|`. The interior angle at `b` is therefore less than `π/2`, so the exterior turn at `b` is greater than `π/2`. The reflected endpoint-centered configuration has the same consequence. Since exterior turns sum to `2π`, at most three boundary positions can carry such a pattern.

Closest existing pieces include:

- `ShellCurvature.openWindow_two_sided_quarter_turn_separation_of_equal_radius`;
- `ShellCurvatureRows.ShellBoundaryIndexing.selectedFourClass_has_globalSelectedQuarterTurnArcs`;
- `ShellCurvature.false_of_four_separated_quarter_turn_arcs`.

The missing reusable layer is a finite-family adapter from selected-row neighbor pairs to four pairwise separated quarter-turn arcs.

#### Fixed-pivot radial-arc budget

For a fixed outside pivot, consistently oriented transitions between equal-radius owner centers with chord equal to the radius advance by `π/3`. If all centers lie in an open semicircle, at most two consecutive transitions can occur.

#### Seven-center equilateral walk

Six consistently oriented `π/3` steps on one positive-radius circle return to the starting point, contradicting distinctness of the first and seventh centers.

Nearest existing fixed-motif/angle infrastructure includes:

- `EqualityCore.seven_point_circle_network_collision`;
- `EqualityCore.seven_point_six_circle_collision`;
- `EqualityCore.six_point_circle_chain_collision`;
- `SixPointCircleChainOrderCore.metric_orientation_incompatibility`;
- `arcAngle_chord_length_eq_iff`;
- `edgeLift_add_period`.

No arbitrary-cardinality theorem currently packages the exact seven-center statement. The missing bridge is: equal radius and equal chord + consistent CCW orientation imply an exact lifted gap of `π/3`, followed by a six-gap period contradiction.

#### Equilateral-rhombus affine identity

When a fixed outside pivot occurs in rows two owner positions apart, the two equilateral triangles produce an affine parallelogram/rhombus identity. This is a promising bridge from row equalities to a small planar rigidity circuit, but no live packet currently supplies it directly.
