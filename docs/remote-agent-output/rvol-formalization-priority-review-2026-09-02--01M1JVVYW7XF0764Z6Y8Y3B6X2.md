## 3. Ranking criteria

Each recommendation is assessed by five questions:

- **Registry effect:** would finishing the stated unit remove a generated obligation rather than add a helper?
- **Ingress readiness:** does a current source packet already provide most hypotheses?
- **Leverage:** how much of the proof spine becomes clean or simpler?
- **Mathematical confidence:** is the proposed statement proved/verified, or still a conjectural producer?
- **Off-spine risk:** how likely is the work to end as an elegant theorem with no live caller?

The ranking deliberately favors complete closure units. A low-level helper can be first in the implementation sequence without being the highest-ranked mathematical objective.
